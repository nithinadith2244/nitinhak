Rules of engagement

Hacking skills are only useful when they are used with consent. These rules apply to every course and every job.

  • Only with permissionNo testing without written authorisation from the owner of the system.
  • Labs, not live targetsStudents practise on intentionally vulnerable environments and their own equipment.
  • Report, do not exploitFindings go to the owner with a fix, and sensitive data is never kept.
  • Teach the defenceEvery attack technique is paired with how to detect and prevent it.
